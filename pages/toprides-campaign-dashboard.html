How this dashboard updates
  1. ITP Live keeps one reporting sheet with three tabs: performance, creator posts and the creator roster.
  2. Each tab is published as CSV and linked in the settings at the top of this page's code.
  3. Every time the page opens it pulls the latest rows. If the sheet can't be reached, it shows the last good pull and says when it was taken.
